Delete your Dueline account

Dueline is made by The Algothrim. You can delete your account and everything in it whenever you like.

In the app

  1. Open Dueline and go to You.
  2. Tap Delete everything and confirm.

It happens straight away. Want a copy first? You > Export my data downloads everything as a file.

By email, if you can't open the app

Write to gaurav@thealgothrim.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Dueline account". Your account and data are deleted within 7 days, and you get a reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Every payment, cycle, payee and history entry
  • Your settings, reminder devices and the log of sent reminders
  • Your login

Nothing is kept afterwards. Dueline holds no backups of personal data, so a deleted account can't be restored.
