Dueline

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Dueline keeps track of the payments you have coming up. It is made by Gaurav Kumar, The Algothrim, in New Delhi, India. This page says what Dueline stores, where it lives, who can see it and how to delete it. Questions go to gaurav@thealgothrim.com.

What Dueline stores

  • The payments you add: names, amounts, due dates and schedules, notes, payment links, card or account labels you type (such as "HDFC ••4821"), and the UPI IDs, names and phone numbers of people you pay.
  • What you mark: paid, skipped, moved or snoozed cycles, the amount and date you paid, and any reference number you enter.
  • Your settings: timezone, reminder times and payday.
  • Your account: if you sign in with Google, your email address, name and profile picture from Google; if you use email, your email address. Guests get an anonymous account with no email.
  • Reminders: if you turn them on, the push address your browser or phone gives Dueline, a label such as "Chrome on Android", and a log of which reminders were sent so none goes out twice.

What Dueline never asks for

No bank logins, card numbers, CVVs or UPI PINs. Dueline does not read your SMS, contacts, location or email, does not show ads, and has no analytics or tracking. When you pay, Dueline opens your own UPI app, bank or the biller's page; the payment happens there, not in Dueline.

Where it lives

  • Google Firebase holds your account and your data, in Google's Mumbai region (asia-south1), and serves the app.
  • The reminder server runs on Vercel, also in Mumbai. Once an hour it reads schedules to work out which reminders are due and writes the "already sent" log. It cannot change your payments.
  • Push services (Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your device) deliver each reminder and see only the notification's text.

These providers process data only to run Dueline. Everything travels over encrypted connections.

Who can see it

Only you. Every record is stored under your account, and the database's security rules refuse anyone else, including other Dueline users. Dueline does not sell, rent or share your data, and would disclose it only if Indian law required it.

Keeping and deleting it

Your data stays until you delete it. You > Export my data downloads everything as a file at any time. You > Delete everything removes every payment, payee, history entry, device and reminder log entry, then the login itself, straight away. If you can't open the app, see how to ask for deletion by email.

Children

Dueline is meant for adults managing their own payments and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date. Changes that affect what is stored will also be announced in the app.
